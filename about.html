TempStitch

Design beautiful temperature blankets from historical weather

About

TempStitch turns a year of daily maximum temperatures into a knitting or crochet pattern. Choose a location, pick a year, and TempStitch fetches historical weather data to generate a colour-coded design and written row-by-row instructions.

No account, no server, no tracking. Everything runs in your browser.

Features

  • Smart colour scaling — ranges auto-calculate from your local climate
  • 5 palettes — gradient, warm, cool, pastel, monochrome
  • Knit or crochet — with UK or US terminology
  • Numbered colours — C1–C12 with names and temperature ranges
  • Size estimation — based on standard gauges for each craft
  • Year selector — choose any year from 1940
  • Download — design image and pattern instructions as text files
  • 100% client-side — no server, no account, no tracking
